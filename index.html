About the artist

Born in Seoul , South Korea Jennie Kim rose to fame globally as a member of the multi-achieving group BLACKPINK before launching her own successful independent label, ODD ATELIER.

Beyond her music , Jennie is a global fashion icon , serving as a house ambassador for Chanel and is fronting major global campaigns. Her sharp visual eye aligns perfectly with the philosophy that " Photography is a way of feeling ,touching, and of loving. What you have caught on film is captured forever." Whether she is being captured by the paparazzi on the runway or filmimg behind-the-scenes moments for her millions of subscribers , she treats style as an eternal art form.
